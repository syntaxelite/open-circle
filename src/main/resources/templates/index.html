About Open Circle & Source Integrity

Honest Purpose, Academic Grounding

Open Circle was conceived and built as an educational project to demonstrate how thoughtfully designed digital health tools can prioritize user dignity, factual accuracy, and strict privacy over commercial advertising or clickbait.

Why Open Circle Exists

Many mental health websites are cluttered with commercial ads, generic text, or unvetted claims. Open Circle demonstrates an editorial, human-centered alternative treating sensitive health topics with respect and care.

Evidence-Based Standards

Every psychoeducational guide is cross-referenced with public documents from the World Health Organization (WHO), National Health Service (NHS England), and National Institute of Mental Health (NIMH).

College Project Demonstration

Built with Java 21, Spring Boot, Maven, and Thymeleaf using pure HTML and CSS without client-side JavaScript. Showcases accessible design, semantic markup, and privacy safeguards.
